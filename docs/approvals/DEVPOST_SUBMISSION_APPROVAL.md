# Devpost Submission Approval

Status: pending approval.

## Scope

Approve preparing and submitting a Devpost package after all local evidence is reviewed.

## Required Evidence Before Approval

- Local export dry-run passes.
- Submission copy reviewed.
- Screenshots and videos reviewed.
- No private repo, secret, customer data, billing, or internal endpoint exposure.

## Blocked Until Approved

- Real submission.
- Public upload.
- External account login automation.

## Approval Phrase

```text
Approve Part 8 Devpost submission only.
```
