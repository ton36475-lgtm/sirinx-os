## Strategy Readout

SIRINX should prioritize clear service packaging, proof-focused trust sections, and a visible approval-gated automation story. Avoid copying competitor UI, private workflows, or unverifiable claims.

## Build Packet

### Opportunity

Create a public offer comparison module that explains what the client receives, what requires human approval, and what is intentionally blocked.

### Risks

- Competitor claims may be marketing copy rather than proof.
- Pricing may vary by client and should not be treated as exact without direct public evidence.
- Any inferred feature must be labeled as inferred.

### Next Build Actions
