# AI Education Carousel Asset Pack

Status: local prompt asset pack only. Not generated, not published, not sent.

## Scope

This pack provides five reusable carousel prompt templates for AI Academy education content:

1. AI Basics Explainer
2. AI Workflow Tutorial
3. Second Brain Lesson
4. Agent Safety Gate
5. Business Automation Offer

## Style Direction

Clean Editorial AI Academy:

- warm ivory base
- deep ink navy typography
- cool slate panels
- clean cyan diagram accents
- warm coral warnings and emphasis
- muted gold module markers

The pack intentionally avoids dark sci-fi styling, intense glow effects, and black-dominant layouts so it stays readable and distinct from Ghost Claws and AGM dark visual systems.
