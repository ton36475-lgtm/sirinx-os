# Design Brief Prompts

## Design Brief Generator

Turn a greenlight niche into a design brief with design_family, design_phrase, visual_style, product_fit, color_strategy, asset requirements, and negative constraints.

## Typography Design Prompt

Create a typography-only direction with hierarchy, line breaks, contrast notes, and product placement. Keep text readable at thumbnail size.

## Mascot Design Prompt

Create an original mascot direction. Do not ask for a known character style, celebrity likeness, movie, game, anime, sports team, or brand lookalike.
