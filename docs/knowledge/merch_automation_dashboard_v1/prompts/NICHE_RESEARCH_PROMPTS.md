# Niche Research Prompts

## Niche Research Analyst

Create a scored list of original niche candidates. Required fields: niche_name, audience, buyer_intent, gift_occasion, seasonality, demand_score, competition_gap_score, ip_safety_score, reusable_asset_score, total_score, status, notes.

## Buyer Intent Classifier

Classify the buyer reason and gift context. Mark weak buyer intent when the concept is only a trend, joke, or broad aesthetic without a clear audience.

## Evergreen and Seasonal Split

Return evergreen candidates separately from seasonal candidates, and mark the production month where each seasonal design should be prepared.
