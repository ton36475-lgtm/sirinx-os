# IP Policy Checklist

## Green

- Original phrase and artwork.
- No brand, team, university, celebrity, lyric, movie, game, anime, or known character dependency.
- No confusing parody or lookalike.

## Yellow

- Generic word may need replacement.
- Visual style too close to a known property.
- Keyword cluster includes risky adjacent terms.

## Red

- Depends on protected reference.
- Celebrity likeness or name.
- Sports team, school, brand, or franchise confusion.
- Song lyric or recognizable quote.

## Final Manual Review

This checklist is a local precheck. It does not provide legal clearance.
