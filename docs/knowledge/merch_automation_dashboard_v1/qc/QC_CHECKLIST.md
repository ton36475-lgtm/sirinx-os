# QC Checklist

## Visual Quality

- Original artwork only.
- Clean silhouette at thumbnail size.
- No known brand layout or protected character style.

## Print Readiness

- Transparent background where product requires it.
- Correct DPI and export dimensions.
- No unintended border, artifact, or pixel debris.

## Text Accuracy

- Spelling checked.
- Grammar and phrase readability checked.
- No accidental protected terms.

## Readability

- Text readable at storefront thumbnail scale.
- Strong hierarchy and spacing.

## Color Contrast

- Works on intended shirt colors.
- No low-contrast thin strokes.

## Product Placement

- Artwork centered and scaled for product type.
- Avoid edges and seams.

## IP/Policy Risk

- IP Guardian status is GREEN or revised YELLOW.
- RED concepts are retired, not reworded to bypass rules.

## Listing SEO

- Natural title and bullets.
- No keyword stuffing.
- No false quality, review, medical, or income claims.

## Traffic Content Safety

- Manual post only.
- No fake engagement.
- No customer impersonation.

## Analytics Logging

- Upload date recorded only after owner action.
- Traffic source tag and iteration decision captured.
