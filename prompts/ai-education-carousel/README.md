# AI Education Carousel Prompt Pack

Status: local prompt templates only. No paid image generation, publishing, or customer send is authorized by this pack.

## Purpose

Create reusable Thai-first carousel prompts for AI Academy education content. The visual direction is clean editorial, bright, readable, and suitable for learning material.

## Files

- `prompts/ai-education-carousel/templates.json`
- `packages/asset-registry/ai-education-carousel/style-tokens.json`
- `packages/asset-registry/ai-education-carousel/thai-typography-checklist.md`
- `docs/creative/AI_EDUCATION_CAROUSEL_ASSET_PACK.md`

## Workflow

1. Pick one template by `id`.
2. Replace headline and slide copy with approved Thai-first content.
3. Run the local validator.
4. Review Thai line breaks and readability manually.
5. Generate images only after a separate media-generation gate.
6. Publish only after a separate public-output approval gate.

## Guardrails

- Keep the warm ivory, deep ink navy, cool slate, clean cyan, warm coral, and muted gold palette.
- Keep hierarchy readable on mobile.
- Do not use dark sci-fi, hard neon, over-glow, or black-dominant visuals.
- Do not make exaggerated learning, revenue, or business outcome claims.
