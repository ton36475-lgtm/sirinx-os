# Local Business Public Marketing Checklist

## Text Accuracy

- [ ] Use the exact locked public name.
- [ ] Keep Thai spelling unchanged.
- [ ] Do not shorten the business name unless an alias is explicitly allowed.
- [ ] Check generated text before public use.

## Visual Scope

- [ ] Public marketing only.
- [ ] Use realistic product/place visuals.
- [ ] Keep the business/service as the first visual signal.
- [ ] Avoid fake chat UI, private system screens, internal dashboards, cyberpunk, cartoon, and anime styles.

## Safety

- [ ] Do not include hidden backend info.
- [ ] Do not include customer data.
- [ ] Do not imply guaranteed income, sales, or outcomes.
- [ ] Do not run paid generation or public posting without owner approval.
