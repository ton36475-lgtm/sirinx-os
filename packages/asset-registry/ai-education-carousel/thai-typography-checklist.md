# Thai Typography Checklist

Use this checklist before generating or publishing an AI Education Carousel.

## Required

- Thai headline is readable at mobile size.
- Thai body copy uses short lines and avoids dense paragraphs.
- No broken Thai words across line breaks.
- No decorative Thai font for body text.
- Punctuation spacing is consistent.
- Numbers and English technical terms are spaced clearly.
- Each slide has one main idea.
- Color contrast is high enough for reading.

## Review Notes

- Prefer Thai-first headline, then short English support only when needed.
- Keep CTA wording neutral and educational.
- Do not promise learning, income, or business outcomes.
- Manual Thai spelling review is still required before public use.
